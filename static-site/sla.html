Service Level Agreement.

The availability, support and shortlist commitments we make to paying CALIBR customers — and the credits that apply if we miss them.

Provider: KTH-Tech (Reg 2025/627290/07), Gauteng, South Africa · enterprise@kth-tech.com · Last updated: 2026

1. Scope

This Service Level Agreement (SLA) applies to customers on a current, paid CALIBR subscription and forms part of our Terms of Use. It describes our targets for availability, support and the 48-hour shortlist commitment, and the remedies if we fall short.

2. Availability

We target 99.5% monthly uptime for the core platform, measured excluding scheduled maintenance and events outside our reasonable control. We aim to give advance notice of scheduled maintenance and to run it in low-traffic windows.

3. The 48-hour shortlist commitment

For a role posted with a complete job description on an eligible plan, we commit to deliver a scored, ranked, parse-safe shortlist of applicants within two business days of the role receiving its first qualifying applications. The commitment covers CALIBR's automated scoring and ranking; it does not guarantee a minimum number of applicants, nor that you will make a hire. If we miss it on an eligible role, your next qualifying role is scored at no additional charge.

4. Support

  • ChannelsEmail at enterprise@kth-tech.com; priority channels for Growth and Enterprise plans.
  • Response targetsCritical (platform down): within 4 business hours. High: 1 business day. Normal: 2 business days.
  • Business hours08:00–17:00 SAST, Monday to Friday, excluding South African public holidays.

5. Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below 99.5%, Growth and Enterprise customers may request a service credit against the next invoice: 5% of the monthly fee for 99.0–99.5%, 10% for 95.0–98.9%, and 20% below 95.0%. Credits are the sole remedy for missed availability, are capped at one month's fee per month, and must be requested within 30 days.

6. Exclusions

The targets above do not apply to: beta or clearly-labelled preview features; issues caused by your systems, data or misuse; third-party services outside our control (including payment, email and board integrations); force majeure; and the honest stubs for features not yet built (external board sourcing, calendar sync, e-signature, live market-comp data and the Academy).

7. Contact

To claim a credit or raise a service issue, contact enterprise@kth-tech.com.
