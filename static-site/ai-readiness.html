The idea

What AI-readiness
really means.

AI-readiness isn't buying a tool licence. It's whether your people know what these tools do well, where they fail, and how to use them without breaking the law or your reputation. It spans four things: skills, tools, governance and data.

Most teams have a few enthusiastic power-users and a lot of quiet uncertainty. We turn that into a clear, shared baseline — so AI becomes a capability across the business, not a handful of side projects.

The maturity path

Every engagement moves your team along a four-stage curve — from ad-hoc experiments to AI as a reliable, governed capability.

L1

Aware.

A few users experimenting. No shared rules, no measurement, uneven results.

L2

Applied.

Teams use AI on real tasks. Basic guidance in place; value is visible but patchy.

L3

Governed.

Approved tools, clear POPIA guardrails, role-based skills. AI is trusted for daily work.

L4

Embedded.

AI is part of how the business runs — measured, safe, and a genuine competitive edge.
