Refund & Cancellation Policy.

How cancellations, renewals and refunds work on CALIBR — written to honour your rights under the Consumer Protection Act and the ECT Act.

Provider: KTH-Tech (Reg 2025/627290/07), Gauteng, South Africa · enterprise@kth-tech.com · Last updated: 2026

1. Subscriptions and renewals

CALIBR plans are billed monthly in advance in South African Rand and renew automatically until cancelled. You authorise us and our payment processor to charge your chosen method on each renewal date. We show the price and renewal date before you subscribe.

2. Cancelling

You may cancel at any time from your account settings or by emailing enterprise@kth-tech.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period — you keep access until then, and recurring billing stops. There is no lock-in and no cancellation penalty.

3. ECT Act cooling-off

Where section 44 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, 2002 applies to an electronic transaction, you may cancel within the statutory cooling-off period and receive a refund of amounts paid, less the direct cost of any service already supplied at your request. Statutory exclusions (for example, a service fully performed, or content already consumed, with your consent) may apply.

4. Consumer Protection Act

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2008 (CPA). Where the CPA gives you a right to cancel, a cooling-off right, or a remedy for a service that is not of a reasonable standard, we will honour it. If a provision of this policy conflicts with the CPA, the CPA prevails to the extent of the conflict.

5. When we will refund

  • Billing errorA duplicate charge, a charge after valid cancellation, or an incorrect amount — refunded in full once confirmed.
  • Service failureA material failure on our side that we cannot remedy within a reasonable time, assessed against our SLA.
  • Statutory rightAny refund required by the CPA or ECT Act.

6. When we will not refund

Except where the law requires it, monthly fees already paid are not refunded on a pro-rata basis for the unused part of a period after you cancel, and we do not refund for change of mind once a service has been delivered or for failure to use the platform.

7. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method, in Rand, normally within 10 business days of approval. Processing times at your bank or card issuer are outside our control.

8. Contact

Refund requests and billing questions: enterprise@kth-tech.com.
