Privacy Policy.

How we collect, use, share and protect your personal information — written to comply with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Last updated: 2026 · Applies to calibr-careers.tech and the CALIBR platform · Responsible party: KTH-Tech (Reg 2025/627290/07), Gauteng, South Africa

1. Who we are

CALIBR is an AI hiring and career-intelligence platform operated by KTH-Tech, a private company registered in the Republic of South Africa under registration number 2025/627290/07, with its registered address in Gauteng, South Africa (we, us, our). For the purposes of the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA), we are the responsible party that determines the purpose of and means for processing personal information collected through CALIBR.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we process, why we process it, the lawful basis for doing so, who we share it with, and the rights you hold as a data subject. It should be read together with our Terms of Use. By creating an account or using CALIBR, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

2. Information Officer

KTH-Tech has appointed an Information Officer as required by POPIA and registered with the Information Regulator. The Information Officer is responsible for encouraging and ensuring compliance with POPIA, dealing with requests made to us, and working with the Regulator.

  • Information OfficerKTH-Tech
  • Emailenterprise@kth-tech.com
  • RegulatorInformation Regulator (South Africa)

3. What personal information we collect

We collect only the personal information reasonably necessary to provide CALIBR. Depending on how you use the platform, this may include:

3.1 Information you give us directly

  • Contact and account detailsName, email address, mobile number, password (stored hashed), and the province or city you work in.
  • Career and application dataYour CV or résumé, cover letters, work history, qualifications, skills, salary expectations, and job preferences.
  • Assessment responsesYour answers and results across our six assessments — Aptitude, Technical, Personality, Culture-Fit, Integrity and Cognitive.
  • Employer informationFor employer accounts: company name, registration details, billing contact, vacancy briefs and hiring criteria.
  • CommunicationsMessages, support requests and feedback you send us.

3.2 Information we generate

  • The CALIBR ScoreA verified, tamper-proof rating derived from your assessment results and verified profile data, scored on the same axes for every candidate.
  • Usage and technical dataLog data, device and browser type, IP address, pages visited, and interactions with the platform, collected to keep CALIBR secure and to improve it.

We do not knowingly collect special personal information (as defined in POPIA) such as race, health, religious belief or biometric data for the purpose of scoring. Where employment-equity or B-BBEE reporting requires demographic data, it is provided voluntarily by the candidate, used only for compliance reporting, and processed in line with section 27 of POPIA and the relevant employment-equity legislation.

4. Lawful basis and consent

We process personal information only where POPIA permits it. Depending on the activity, our lawful basis is one or more of the following:

  • ConsentYou have given voluntary, specific and informed consent — for example, when you complete an assessment or agree to share your profile with a named employer.
  • Performance of a contractProcessing is necessary to deliver the service you subscribed to.
  • Legitimate interestProcessing serves our or a third party's legitimate interest — such as securing the platform or preventing fraud — balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligationProcessing is required to comply with a law, including tax, employment-equity and record-keeping obligations.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time by contacting the Information Officer. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing carried out before the withdrawal, and may mean we can no longer provide parts of the service.

5. Why we process your information

  • To operate CALIBRCreate and manage your account, run assessments, generate your CALIBR Score, and match candidates to roles.
  • To help you get hired or hireSurface relevant vacancies, optimise applications, and let employers screen and shortlist against verified criteria.
  • To support complianceGenerate B-BBEE and employment-equity reports for employers where the candidate has consented.
  • To bill and administerProcess monthly subscriptions in Rand, and keep the records the law requires.
  • To secure and improveDetect misuse, debug, and develop new features.
  • To communicateSend service messages and, where you have opted in, product updates.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your CALIBR Score or assessment data for automated decisions that produce a legal or similarly significant effect on you without a human being able to review that decision — the Score is decision-support for people, not a substitute for their judgement.

6. The CALIBR Score and candidate data

The CALIBR Score is central to the platform. It is calculated from your own assessment results and verified profile information, and is designed to be comparable across candidates because everyone is scored on the same axes. Your Score belongs to your profile: it is not shared with an employer until you apply to that employer or expressly consent to being included in their talent pool. You may re-take assessments in line with platform rules, and you can request that your Score and underlying data be corrected or deleted as set out in section 9.

7. Sharing with employers and third parties

We share personal information only as needed to provide CALIBR and only with appropriate safeguards:

  • EmployersWhen you apply for a role or consent to join a talent pool, the relevant employer receives your profile, application materials and CALIBR Score.
  • OperatorsVetted service providers (for example, cloud hosting, email delivery and payment processing) who process personal information on our behalf under written contracts requiring POPIA-compliant security and confidentiality.
  • Legal and safetyWhere we must comply with a law, court order or lawful request, or to protect our rights and the safety of users.
  • Business transfersIn connection with a merger, acquisition or reorganisation, subject to this policy continuing to apply.

Employers who receive candidate data through CALIBR act as independent responsible parties for their own use of that data and are contractually required to handle it lawfully.

8. Cross-border transfers and data residency

We aim to host and process personal information on infrastructure located in, or serving, South Africa. Where a service provider processes personal information outside the Republic, we transfer it only in accordance with section 72 of POPIA — that is, to a recipient bound by binding rules or a contract that upholds a level of protection substantially similar to POPIA, or where you have consented, or where the transfer is necessary to perform our contract with you. Our primary cloud and email providers include AWS and Microsoft, with whom we maintain data-protection terms.

9. Your rights under POPIA

As a data subject you have the right to:

  • AccessAsk what personal information we hold about you and request a copy.
  • CorrectionAsk us to correct or update information that is inaccurate, misleading or out of date.
  • DeletionAsk us to delete or destroy information we no longer have a lawful basis to keep.
  • ObjectionObject, on reasonable grounds, to the processing of your information.
  • Withdraw consentWithdraw consent you previously gave, on a going-forward basis.
  • ComplainLodge a complaint with the Information Regulator, or submit a civil claim to a court.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Information Officer at enterprise@kth-tech.com. We may ask you to verify your identity, and we will respond within a reasonable period as required by POPIA. Requests for access may be made using the prescribed form under the Promotion of Access to Information Act, 2000 (PAIA).

10. Security safeguards

We maintain appropriate, reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access and misuse, as required by section 19 of POPIA. These include encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access controls on a need-to-know basis, activity logging, and contractual security obligations on our operators. If a security compromise occurs that affects your personal information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as soon as reasonably possible in line with section 22 of POPIA.

11. Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described here, or for as long as the law requires. Account and career data are retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards to allow reactivation and to meet record-keeping obligations. Billing records are retained for the periods required by South African tax and company law. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

12. Minors and consent

CALIBR is intended for use by adults in the workforce and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly process the personal information of a child (a person under 18) without the consent of a competent person, as required by POPIA. If you believe a child has provided us information without such consent, please contact the Information Officer and we will take steps to delete it.

13. Cookies and similar technologies

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and the platform secure, and limited analytics to understand how CALIBR is used so we can improve it. You can control non-essential cookies through your browser settings; blocking necessary cookies may affect how the platform works. We do not use cookies to sell your data or to build advertising profiles.

14. Electronic communications

Any electronic communications and transactions on CALIBR are subject to the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, 2002 (ECT Act). Marketing communications are sent only where you have opted in or the law permits, and every such message includes an easy way to opt out. Our required disclosures under section 43 of the ECT Act appear on our website and in these documents.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the platform or the law. When we make material changes, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you. Your continued use of CALIBR after an update means you accept the revised policy.

16. Contact us

For any privacy question, request or complaint, contact the Information Officer of KTH-Tech at enterprise@kth-tech.com. You may also lodge a complaint directly with the Information Regulator (South Africa).
