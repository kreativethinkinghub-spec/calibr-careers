Six reasoning domains

One sitting, six ways of thinking. Together they show employers how you handle new problems — not just what you already know.

01

Numerical.

Work with numbers, ratios, percentages and data tables under time pressure — the core of finance, analytics and operations roles.

02

Verbal.

Read closely, judge arguments and draw the right conclusion from a passage. Essential wherever clear thinking on the page matters.

03

Logical.

Spot the rule, follow the sequence, and reason from premises to a sound answer — the backbone of problem-solving at work.

04

Spatial.

Rotate, fold and picture shapes in your head. Strong signals for engineering, design, trades and technical fields.

05

Abstract.

Find the pattern in unfamiliar symbols — a fair test of raw reasoning that doesn't depend on your schooling or background.

06

Mechanical.

Apply everyday physics — levers, gears, pressure and force — to practical problems in artisan, manufacturing and maintenance roles.
