PAIA Manual.

Our manual under section 51 of the Promotion of Access to Information Act, 2000 — how to request access to records held by KTH-Tech.

Provider: KTH-Tech (Reg 2025/627290/07), Gauteng, South Africa · enterprise@kth-tech.com · Last updated: 2026

1. Introduction

This manual is published under section 51 of the Promotion of Access to Information Act, 2000 (PAIA) for KTH-Tech (Reg 2025/627290/07), the operator of CALIBR (the company). It explains the records we hold and how to request access to them.

2. Contact details

  • Head of the private bodyThe Director, KTH-Tech
  • Information OfficerVia enterprise@kth-tech.com
  • Postal / registered addressGauteng, South Africa
  • Emailenterprise@kth-tech.com

3. The PAIA Guide

The Information Regulator has published a Guide on how to use PAIA, available in each official language from the Regulator. It explains your rights and how to exercise them. Contact: the Information Regulator (South Africa), inforeg@justice.gov.za.

4. Records held

  • CompanyRegistration, statutory, financial, tax and contractual records.
  • Customers & usersAccount, subscription, billing and support records; candidate profiles, assessments and CALIBR Scores processed on the platform.
  • OperationsProduct, security, personnel and supplier records.
  • Other lawsRecords kept under the Companies Act, Tax Administration Act, POPIA and labour legislation.

5. How to request access

Submit a request on the prescribed PAIA Form 2 (Request for Access to Record of Private Body) to the Information Officer by email. Identify yourself, identify the record sought, state the right you seek to exercise, and give a contact address. We will respond within 30 days, which may be extended as PAIA allows.

6. Fees

A request fee and access fee may be payable as prescribed by the PAIA regulations. We will notify you of any fee before processing, and may require it to be paid before access is given.

7. Grounds for refusal

Access may be refused on the grounds set out in Chapter 4 of Part 3 of PAIA, including the mandatory protection of the privacy of a third party, commercial information, and records privileged from production in legal proceedings.

8. Protection of personal information

Processing of personal information by the company is governed by POPIA and our Privacy Policy. Data subjects may exercise their POPIA rights — including access, correction and deletion — as set out there.
