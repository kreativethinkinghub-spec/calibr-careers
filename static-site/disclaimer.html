1. Decision-support, not a guarantee

CALIBR assessments and the CALIBR Score are decision-support tools. They help candidates present their ability and help employers make better-informed decisions. They do not guarantee that a candidate will be hired, that an employer will fill a role, or that any person will perform in a given job. Employers remain responsible for their own hiring decisions and for complying with all labour, equity and privacy law.

Any market statistics, time-to-hire benchmarks, cost comparisons or recruiter-fee figures shown on this website or in the product are illustrative market benchmarks drawn from third-party sources. They are not CALIBR results, not promises of outcome, and your results will vary.
