Data Processing Agreement.

The POPIA terms on which CALIBR processes candidate personal information on an employer’s behalf — the employer is the responsible party; CALIBR is the operator.

Provider: KTH-Tech (Reg 2025/627290/07), Gauteng, South Africa · enterprise@kth-tech.com · Last updated: 2026

1. Roles under POPIA

This Data Processing Agreement (DPA) applies where an employer customer (Responsible Party) uses CALIBR to process candidate personal information. KTH-Tech (operating CALIBR) acts as the Operator under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA), processing personal information only on the Responsible Party's documented instructions, which include use of the platform's features. This DPA forms part of the Terms of Use.

2. Subject-matter and nature of processing

  • PurposeScreening, scoring, ranking and shortlisting candidates, and generating employment-equity and B-BBEE reporting.
  • Categories of data subjectJob applicants and candidates.
  • Categories of personal informationIdentity and contact details, CV and work history, assessment answers and results, the CALIBR Score, and — where the candidate provides it for EE purposes — demographic information (special personal information handled under section 26 and the EE Act).

3. Operator obligations

We will: process personal information only on the Responsible Party's lawful instructions; keep it confidential and ensure persons authorised to process it are bound by confidentiality; apply the security safeguards in section 19 of POPIA and our Security Policy; assist the Responsible Party, as far as reasonably possible, with data-subject requests, security-compromise notifications and its POPIA duties; and not process personal information for our own purposes beyond providing and improving the service in de-identified or aggregate form.

4. Sub-operators

The Responsible Party authorises our use of sub-operators for hosting, payments and communications. We impose data-protection obligations on each sub-operator no less protective than this DPA, and remain responsible for their performance. Current categories include cloud hosting, our payment processor and our messaging/email providers. We will inform the Responsible Party of material changes to sub-operators on request.

5. Cross-border transfers

Personal information is hosted with a preference for South African or POPIA-adequate data residency. Any transfer outside South Africa is made only on a basis permitted by section 72 of POPIA (such as adequate protection, consent, or necessity for the contract). See our Privacy Policy for current residency.

6. Security compromises

We will notify the Responsible Party without undue delay after becoming aware of a security compromise affecting their candidate data, with the information needed for the Responsible Party to meet its notification duties to the Information Regulator and affected data subjects under section 22 of POPIA.

7. Return and deletion

On termination, or on the Responsible Party's instruction, we will delete or return candidate personal information processed on its behalf, except where retention is required by law. De-identified or aggregate data that no longer identifies any person may be retained.

8. Audit and contact

We will make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and, on reasonable notice and confidentiality terms, support a proportionate audit. DPA queries: enterprise@kth-tech.com.
