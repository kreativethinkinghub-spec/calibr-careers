Acceptable Use Policy.

The rules for using CALIBR fairly, lawfully and safely. Breaching this policy can lead to suspension or termination.

Provider: KTH-Tech (Reg 2025/627290/07), Gauteng, South Africa · enterprise@kth-tech.com · Last updated: 2026

1. Scope

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) applies to everyone who uses CALIBR — job seekers, employers, partners and administrators — and forms part of our Terms of Use. By using the platform you agree to it.

2. You must not

  • MisrepresentProvide false information, impersonate another person, or falsify assessment answers, a CV or a CALIBR Score.
  • Misuse personal informationUse candidate or employer personal information for any purpose other than the lawful hiring purpose for which it was shared, in breach of POPIA.
  • Discriminate unlawfullyUse the platform to screen, rank or reject candidates in breach of the Employment Equity Act, the Constitution or other equality law.
  • Attack or probeIntroduce malware, attempt to gain unauthorised access, scan, overload, or interfere with the platform's security or availability.
  • Reverse-engineer or scrapeCopy, scrape, resell, reverse-engineer or attempt to game the scoring engine, or bypass access controls, rate limits or the API terms.
  • Infringe or offendUpload content you have no right to use, or anything unlawful, defamatory, hateful or harmful.

3. Employer obligations

Employers must have a lawful basis to process candidate data, use CALIBR outputs only as decision-support alongside human judgement, and keep hiring decisions compliant with South African labour and equity law. The CALIBR Score and assessments do not replace an employer's own lawful, fair decision-making.

4. Automated access and the API

Access through our REST API and webhooks must stay within the issued key's scope and published rate limits. Keys are confidential; you are responsible for activity under your keys and must rotate or revoke a key you believe is compromised.

5. Enforcement

We may investigate suspected breaches, remove offending content, throttle or suspend access, revoke API keys, and terminate accounts where a breach is found — giving notice where practicable. Serious or unlawful conduct may be reported to the relevant authorities. Nothing here limits our other rights under the Terms of Use.

6. Reporting abuse

Report misuse, security issues or policy breaches to enterprise@kth-tech.com. Security researchers: see our Security Policy for responsible-disclosure.
