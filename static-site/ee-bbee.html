Employment Equity & B-BBEE Statement.

How CALIBR handles demographic data for employment-equity and B-BBEE reporting — lawfully, with consent, and never to discriminate unfairly.

Provider: KTH-Tech (Reg 2025/627290/07), Gauteng, South Africa · enterprise@kth-tech.com · Last updated: 2026

1. Why this data exists on CALIBR

South African employers have duties under the Employment Equity Act, 1998 (EE Act) and the B-BBEE framework to report on workforce demographics and to advance equity. CALIBR helps employers meet these duties by letting candidates optionally provide demographic information and by generating EE and B-BBEE reports and CSV exports.

2. Special personal information

Demographic information such as race, gender and disability is special personal information under POPIA. We process it only where a lawful basis applies — including the candidate's consent and the specific authorisation in POPIA and the EE Act for employment-equity purposes — and only for EE and B-BBEE reporting, never as an unlawful screening criterion.

3. It is optional for candidates

Providing demographic information is voluntary. Declining does not affect a candidate's CALIBR Score or their standing in a selection process. The CALIBR Score is computed from skills, aptitude, personality, culture-fit and integrity evidence — not from demographic data.

4. Fairness safeguards

CALIBR includes an adverse-impact (four-fifths rule) fairness check so employers can monitor whether selection rates differ materially across groups. These tools support lawful, fair hiring and human oversight; they do not authorise or excuse unfair discrimination, which remains unlawful under the Constitution, the EE Act and the Labour Relations Act.

5. Access, use and retention

Demographic data is accessible only to the employer for whom it was provided and to authorised CALIBR personnel for support, is used only for EE/B-BBEE purposes, and is retained no longer than needed for those purposes or as the law requires. Exports are the employer's responsibility to handle under POPIA.

6. Your rights and contact

Candidates may withdraw consent and request correction or deletion of demographic data as set out in our Privacy Policy. Questions: enterprise@kth-tech.com.
