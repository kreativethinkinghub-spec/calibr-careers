How it works

Light-touch, private, and always in your control.

Step 1

Set your rhythm.

Choose how often you want to check in and what a good week looks like for you. No pressure, no leaderboard.

Step 2

Check in.

A quick, private pulse each week. CALIBR reflects your momentum back and offers a small, practical next step.

Step 3

Adjust and keep going.

Ease off when you need to, push when you're ready. The goal is a steady search you can sustain to the finish.
