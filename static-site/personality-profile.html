What we read

Validated, work-relevant traits — not a horoscope. Each one told back to you in plain language you can use in an interview.

01

Communication.

How you share, listen and land a point — from direct and concise to considered and detailed. See where your natural style shines and where to flex.

  • StyleDirect or diplomatic
  • ChannelWritten or in the room
  • InfluenceHow you persuade
02

Handling pressure.

Your response to deadlines, ambiguity and setbacks. Employers value composure — this shows how you keep it and where you recharge.

  • ResilienceBounce-back under load
  • SteadinessCalm in ambiguity
  • RecoveryWhat resets you
03

Team style.

How you contribute in a group — leading, building, connecting or delivering. Find the roles and teams where your default is an asset, not a mismatch.

  • CollaborationSolo or shoulder-to-shoulder
  • DriveInitiate or refine
  • OpennessNew ideas vs proven ways
