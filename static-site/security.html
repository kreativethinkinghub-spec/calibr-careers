Security & Breach-Notification Policy.

The safeguards we apply to protect personal information on CALIBR, how we respond to incidents, and how to report a vulnerability.

Provider: KTH-Tech (Reg 2025/627290/07), Gauteng, South Africa · enterprise@kth-tech.com · Last updated: 2026

1. Our commitment

We apply appropriate, reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access and unlawful processing, as required by section 19 of POPIA. Security is built into how CALIBR is designed and operated.

2. Technical safeguards

  • EncryptionData encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest where supported by our hosting.
  • Access controlRole-based access (seeker, employer, partner, admin), least-privilege, and authenticated sessions.
  • SecretsAPI keys, signing keys and payment credentials held as host environment secrets, never in source control; keys can be rotated and revoked.
  • HardeningSecurity headers (HSTS, CSP, COOP), webhook signature verification, and server-side input validation.
  • PaymentsProcessed by our PCI-DSS-compliant payment processor; we do not store card numbers.

3. Organisational safeguards

Access to personal information is limited to personnel who need it, under confidentiality obligations. We review access, keep audit trails for sensitive actions, and apply secure-development practices including code review and dependency management.

4. Incident response

We maintain an incident-response procedure: detect and contain, assess scope and impact, remediate, and record lessons learned. Suspected compromises are triaged on discovery and escalated to the Information Officer.

5. Breach notification

Where there are reasonable grounds to believe personal information has been accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person, we will notify the Information Regulator and affected data subjects as soon as reasonably possible after establishing the scope, in line with section 22 of POPIA. Where we act as Operator for an employer, we will notify that employer without undue delay so it can meet its own notification duties — see our DPA.

6. Responsible disclosure

We welcome reports from security researchers. Email enterprise@kth-tech.com with details and steps to reproduce. Please act in good faith, avoid privacy violations and service disruption, and give us reasonable time to remediate before public disclosure. We will not pursue good-faith research conducted within these guidelines.

7. Your part

Keep your credentials and API keys confidential, use a strong unique password, and tell us immediately at enterprise@kth-tech.com if you suspect unauthorised access to your account.
