About CALIBR

Built for how South Africa hires and grows.

We're a Gauteng team fixing a broken market: 46% of our young people are out of work while employers say they can't find talent. The problem isn't the people — it's the instruments we hire with. CALIBR calibrates them.

The thesis

Hiring runs on uncalibrated instruments — gut feel, inflated CVs, and interviews that measure confidence, not ability. When every recruiter reads the same candidate differently, nobody can trust the reading. CALIBR is the standard.

01

The problem.

A CV is a claim, not a measurement. An interview scores the room, not the role. Screening 200 applications by hand takes weeks — so good candidates are missed and bad hires slip through at R200k a time.

02

The fix.

Score everyone on the same axes — Aptitude, Technical, Personality, Culture-Fit, Integrity and Cognitive — with AI doing the bulk screening. One calibrated interview replaces five guesses. Time-to-hire drops from 42 days to 8.

03

The standard.

A verified, tamper-proof CALIBR Score means the same number means the same thing for every candidate, at every company. Calibrated once, trusted everywhere — that shared measure is the whole point.

The moat

The CALIBR Score is a verified, tamper-proof rating scored on the same axes for everyone — so it's comparable and trusted. The pre-scored talent pool is what the whole platform is built on.

Verified

Not self-reported.

Six assessments, proctored and locked. A Score can't be inflated on a CV or coached in a room — it's earned once and stands on its own.

Comparable

Same axes, everyone.

Every candidate is measured against the same six dimensions, so a 78 in Cape Town means the same as a 78 in Polokwane. That's what makes it a standard.

Reusable

Score once, apply anywhere.

Candidates carry their Score between applications; employers hire from a pool that's already been measured. Every new user makes the pool more valuable — that's the moat.
