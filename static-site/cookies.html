Cookie Policy.

How CALIBR uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you control them — consistent with POPIA and the ECT Act.

Provider: KTH-Tech (Reg 2025/627290/07), Gauteng, South Africa · enterprise@kth-tech.com · Last updated: 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and software development kits. We use them to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, keep the platform secure, and understand how CALIBR is used so we can improve it.

2. The categories we use

  • Strictly necessarySign-in sessions, security, load-balancing and remembering your cookie choice. The platform cannot work without these, so they are always on.
  • PreferenceRemember settings such as your hub, language or display choices.
  • AnalyticsHelp us understand which features are used so we can improve them. Used only with your consent, and configured to minimise personal information.
  • PaymentSet by our payment processor (Paystack) to process subscriptions securely. We do not store your card details.

We do not use cookies for third-party advertising or to build advertising profiles of you.

3. Your consent and control

Where the law requires consent, non-essential cookies are set only after you accept them. You can withdraw or change your choice at any time by clearing cookies in your browser or using the cookie controls on the site. Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies in settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the platform from working.

4. Retention

Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies last only as long as needed for their purpose, and no longer than is reasonable. You can delete them sooner at any time.

5. Changes and contact

We may update this Cookie Policy as the platform or the law changes. For questions, contact our Information Officer at enterprise@kth-tech.com. This policy should be read with our Privacy Policy.
