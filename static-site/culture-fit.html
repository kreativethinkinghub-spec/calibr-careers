What Culture-Fit does

A structured read on how you work and what you value — matched to the environments where South African teams actually operate.

01

Your values map.

Answer a short, scenario-based assessment and get a clear picture of what drives you — autonomy vs structure, pace vs precision, collaboration vs independence.

  • Work valuesWhat you optimise for
  • Behaviour signalsHow you show up daily
  • Deal-breakersWhere friction starts
02

Company profiles.

Employers on CALIBR define their culture on the same axes you're scored on. That means a like-for-like comparison — not a hunch from a job ad.

  • Same axesComparable, not vague
  • Team realityPace, structure, style

Your fit read.

See a measured fit percentage per role, the reasons behind it, and the gaps worth a conversation in the interview — so you apply where you belong.

How it works

Three steps, then it's part of your Score for every application you send.

Step 1

Complete the assessment.

A 12-minute, scenario-based questionnaire. No trick questions — just honest choices about how you like to work.

Step 2

Match to roles.

CALIBR compares your profile to every open role's culture profile and ranks them by measured fit.

Step 3

Apply with confidence.

Your fit read travels with your CALIBR Score, so employers see a verified, comparable candidate — you.
