What Academy is

A Score without a next step is just a diagnosis. CALIBR Academy is the treatment: certification-aligned training mapped directly to the six axes, so a candidate can see a weak Technical or Cognitive result and know precisely which course closes it. Every track leads to a recognised industry certification, every course ships with an exam voucher, and from Q3 2027 the training will be MICT SETA SDL-claimable — turning your skills-development levy into real, verifiable capability. It's the missing link between knowing where you stand and standing somewhere better.
