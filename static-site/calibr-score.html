What the Score is

The CALIBR Score is a verified, tamper-proof rating of a candidate's ability — scored on the same axes for everyone. Because the measurement never changes from one person to the next, two Scores are directly comparable. A 78 in Cape Town means exactly what a 78 in Polokwane means. That is what makes it trusted: it is not a CV claim, a reference call, or a recruiter's opinion — it is evidence, captured under the same conditions and locked so it cannot be edited after the fact.
