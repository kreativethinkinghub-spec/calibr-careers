POPIA-first by design

Recruitment is one of the most data-heavy processes a business runs. We treat candidate information as special personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act — and build every control around lawful, minimal, accountable handling.

01

Lawful basis.

We map where personal information enters, moves and leaves your hiring workflow, and tie each step to a lawful ground for processing — consent, contract or legitimate interest.

  • Data mappingEnd-to-end candidate flow
  • Consent recordsAuditable, timestamped
02

Minimisation.

Collect only what the role needs, keep it only as long as the law allows, and delete on a defined retention schedule. Less data held is less data at risk.

  • Retention rulesPer record type
  • Right to deletionHonoured on request
03

Accountability.

Clear roles for your Information Officer, documented safeguards, and an incident plan that meets the Act's notification duties to the Information Regulator and affected people.

  • Info Officer supportDuties & register
  • Breach response72-hour posture

Why it matters

Candidate data is a
target.

A single job advert can pull in hundreds of CVs — each one a bundle of ID numbers, addresses, salary history and references. That's exactly the data attackers and fraudsters want. A leak isn't just a breach; under POPIA it can mean regulator enforcement, fines and the loss of every candidate's trust.

Protecting applicants is also a hiring advantage: the best people share more when they know their information is safe. Security is part of your employer brand.
